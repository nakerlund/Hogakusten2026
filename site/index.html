CRA Readiness Check

Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847) self-assessment for production companies placing products with digital elements on the EU market. By Sigma Connectivity.

Key dates: vulnerability and incident reporting to ENISA/CSIRTs applies from 11 Sep 2026; conformity assessment bodies from 11 Jun 2026; all other obligations, including CE marking, from 11 Dec 2027.

Any product with a data connection (direct or indirect) to a device or network is likely in scope. Exceptions exist, e.g. products already covered by medical-device, automotive or aviation rules, and non-commercial open source.

1. Does the CRA apply to you?

Production companies that integrate connected machines, gateways or controllers are normally manufacturers of the final product and must also vet the components they build in. This is guidance, confirm borderline cases with legal counsel.
